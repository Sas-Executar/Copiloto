# Renderer Profiles

Renderers are visual profiles, not semantic schemas.

## Available conceptual profiles

- `editorial` — report hierarchy, whitespace, thin rules.
- `canvas` — business/model canvas blocks.
- `gantt` — timeline grid with phases and milestones.
- `kanban` — state columns with WIP.
- `roadmap` — horizon-based sequencing.
- `journey` — stages × dimensions.
- `mirror` — external experience vs internal operations.
- `blueprint` — customer/frontstage/backstage/support swimlanes.
- `matrix` — comparison or prioritization grid.
- `scorecard` — KPI/OKR structured review.
- `workbook` — guided prompts and fillable sections.
- `memo` — executive narrative document.
- `email` — sendable business communication.
- `register` — structured risk/decision/issue log.
- `procedure` — stepwise SOP layout.
- `spec` — requirements and acceptance criteria.
- `review` — business review with KPIs and implications.

## Rule

The renderer must never invent semantic fields that the chosen template does not have.

The semantic template must never require a specific renderer unless the information itself is inherently spatial or temporal.
